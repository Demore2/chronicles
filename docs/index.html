Chronicles

History, one chapter at a time — the people and events that shaped the world, written to be read on a phone.

Chronicles is a reading app for Android. Twenty stories, eight chapters each, every chapter opening with an illustrated scene. Read offline, finish a story, unlock the figure behind it.
