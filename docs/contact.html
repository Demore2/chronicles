Account & data management requests

What can we help you with?

You can delete your account inside the app. Open Histora and go to Profile → Settings → Delete account. That removes your account and everything stored with it straight away, on the server and on your device. Use this form if you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in.

Privacy. Requests are handled under the GDPR and answered within 30 days. What the app stores is described in the privacy policy.
