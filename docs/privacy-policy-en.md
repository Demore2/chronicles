# Histora — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 14 August 2026*

**In short:** Histora keeps an account and your reading progress, so you can carry on where you
left off on any device. We don't track you, we run no analytics, we show no ads, and we never sell
your data.

## Who we are

Histora is a reading app about historical figures and events, made by Quinten Raats. Under the
GDPR we are the controller of your data. Questions: **quintenraats@gmail.com**.

## What we store

When you create an account, we store:

- **your e-mail address** — to sign you in, confirm your account and let you recover your password;
- **your username** — the name shown in the app, chosen by you;
- **your language preference** — so the app opens in your language;
- **your reading progress** — stories opened, chapters completed, characters unlocked and your
  reading streak.

That is everything. We never ask for your real name, address, phone number, location or payment
details, and the app does not read your contacts, files, photos or camera.

## What we don't do

No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, no tracking cookies, no advertising
identifiers and no profiling. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with third parties for their
own purposes. This version contains no advertising; if advertising is ever added, this policy will
be updated before that version is published.

## Where your data lives

On your own device, and in our database at Supabase, hosted on servers inside the European Union.
Supabase processes the data only on our instructions. Everything travels over an encrypted
connection (HTTPS) and is stored encrypted; database rules make sure your rows can only be read by
your own account.

## Why we may store it

To provide the service you asked for: an account whose progress follows you between devices
(Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract).

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. After deletion, your account and everything linked to it is removed;
encrypted backups still holding a copy are rotated out within 30 days.

## Deleting your account

E-mail **quintenraats@gmail.com** from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your
account and all its data within 30 days — usually much sooner. Uninstalling the app removes the copy
on your device, but leaves the account on the server, so send that mail if you want it gone
entirely.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object
to its use, or take it elsewhere. Mail us and we answer within one month, free of charge. If our
answer doesn't satisfy you, you can complain to your national data protection authority.

## Children

Histora is suitable for all ages. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no purchases.

## Changes to this policy

Any change is published on this page with a new date at the top. The version that applies to you is
the one published here.
