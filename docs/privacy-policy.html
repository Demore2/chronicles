Privacy Policy

Chronicles — last updated 13 August 2026

In short: Chronicles collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything the app remembers about you stays on your own device.

Who we are

Chronicles is an offline reading app about historical figures and events, published for Android on Google Play. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

Data we collect

None. Chronicles does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. In particular, the app does not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location;
  • use analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs;
  • use cookies, advertising identifiers or any other tracking technology;
  • share anything with third parties, because there is nothing to share.

Data stored on your device

To keep your place in a story, the app saves a small amount of information in its own private storage on your device:

  • which chapters you have completed and which stories you have opened;
  • which characters you have unlocked;
  • your reading streak;
  • your theme (light, dark or system) and language preference.

This information never leaves your device and is not readable by us or by anyone else. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Notifications

Chronicles can send you one optional reminder a day, at 7 pm, to read your next chapter. It is off unless you turn it on, you can switch it off again at any time under Profile → Settings → Daily reminder, and it is scheduled entirely by your own device. No push service is involved, nothing is sent to or from a server, and we have no way of knowing whether the reminder appeared or whether you opened it.

Permissions

None of the permissions Chronicles requests give access to your data, your files, your contacts or your location:

  • VIBRATE — the short haptic feedback when you tap a chapter or unlock a character. A normal permission; it asks for no confirmation.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — needed to show the daily reminder described above. Android asks you for this one, and only after you have finished your first chapter. If you say no, the rest of the app is unaffected.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and WAKE_LOCK — let a reminder you enabled survive a restart of your phone and arrive on time. Without them the reminder would silently stop after every reboot.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — part of the app framework. Chronicles works fully offline and contacts no server of ours; see below.

The notification component that schedules the daily reminder is built on Google's messaging library, so the app also declares com.google.android.c2dm.permission.RECEIVE, the permission an app needs to receive push messages. Chronicles sends no push messages and is not registered to receive any. There is no messaging configuration in the app, no push token is ever requested, and the reminder is scheduled by your own device's alarm clock. The permission is listed because the library declares it, not because the app uses it.

Internet access

All story text and all images are bundled inside the app, so Chronicles works fully offline. The app does not contact any server of ours, and we operate no server that receives data from it.

Children

Chronicles is suitable for all ages and contains no user-generated content, no chat and no purchases. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Advertising

This version of Chronicles contains no advertising. If advertising or a paid subscription is ever added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page with a new date at the top. The version that applies to you is the one published here.
